Problem set 8: Random vectors. Distributions derived from Normal distribution

During the seminars, we will work on problems of three different difficulty levels. Category 1 (green) contains introductory problems, Category 2 (blue) contains standard problems, and Category 3 (orange) contains the most challenging problems.

1 Category 1

TipProblem 1

Cars independently pass a point on a busy road at an average rate of 150 per hour.

  1. Assuming a Poisson distribution, find the probability that none passes in one minute.
  2. What is the expected number of cars passing in two minutes?
  3. Find the probability that exactly the expected number of cars passes in a two-minute period.

Other motor vehicles (vans, motorcycles, etc.) pass the same point independently at an average rate of 75 per hour.

  1. Find the probability that exactly one car and one other motor vehicle pass during a two-minute period.
TipProblem 2

Telephone calls enter a hotel switchboard according to a Poisson process at an average rate of two every three minutes.

Let \(Y\) denote the waiting time until the second call.

  1. Find the probability that exactly one call arrives during three minutes.
  2. Find the probability that no more than one call arrives during three minutes.
  3. Compute \(P(Y>3)\).
TipProblem 3

Suppose that the heights (in cm) of Russian adults satisfy

  • Men: \(X\sim N(174.9,7.39^2)\)
  • Women: \(Y\sim N(161.3,6.85^2)\)

Assume independence.

Find the probability that a randomly selected man is at most \(10\) cm taller than a randomly selected woman.

TipProblem 4

Let \(X_1,\ldots,X_8\) be i.i.d. \(N(0,16)\), \(Y_1,\ldots,Y_5\) be i.i.d. \(N(1,9)\), \(Z_1,\ldots,Z_4\) be i.i.d. \(N(0,1)\), all independent.

  1. \(P\!\left(\sum_{i=1}^{4}Z_i^2<5\right)\)

  2. Find \(k\) such that

\[ P\!\left(\sum_{i=1}^{4}Z_i^2<k\right)=0.4. \]

  1. \(P\!\left(\sum_{i=1}^{8}X_i^2<20\right)\)

  2. \(P\!\left(\sum_{i=1}^{5}(Y_i-1)^2>32\right)\)

TipProblem 5

Let \(X_1,\ldots,X_8\) be i.i.d. \(N(0,16)\) and \(Y_1,\ldots,Y_5\) be i.i.d. \(N(1,9)\), all independent.

  1. \(P\!\left(X_1<\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{5}(Y_i-1)^2}\right)\)

  2. \(P\!\left(X_1+2X_2<\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{4}(Y_i-1)^2}\right)\)

  3. \(P\!\left(Y_1-1<\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{8}X_i^2}\right)\)

TipProblem 6

Let \(X_1,\ldots,X_8\) be i.i.d. \(N(0,16)\) and \(Y_1,\ldots,Y_5\) be i.i.d. \(N(1,9)\), all independent.

  1. \(P\!\left(\sum_{i=1}^{8}X_i^2<\sum_{i=1}^{5}(Y_i-1)^2\right)\)

  2. \(P\!\left(\sum_{i=1}^{4}X_i^2<7\sum_{i=1}^{3}(Y_i-1)^2\right)\)

  3. \(P\!\left(\sum_{i=1}^{3}X_i^2<\sum_{i=4}^{8}X_i^2\right)\)

  4. \(P\!\left(\sum_{i=1}^{7}X_i^2<\sum_{i=3}^{8}X_i^2\right)\)

TipProblem 7

Suppose \(X_1,X_2\) are independent \(N(0,4)\) random variables.

Compute

\[ P(X_1^2<36.84-X_2^2). \]

TipProblem 8

Suppose \(X_1,X_2,X_3\) are independent \(N(0,1)\) random variables and \(Y\sim\chi^2_5\) is independent.

\[ P(X_1^2+X_2^2<7.236Y-X_3^2). \]

TipProblem 9

Suppose \(X_i\sim N(0,3)\), \(i=1,\ldots,4\), are independent.

Find \(k\) in each case.

  1. \(P(X_1+3X_2>4)=k\)

  2. \(P(X_1^2+X_2^2+X_3^2+X_4^2<k)=0.99\)

  3. \(P\!\left(X_1<k\sqrt{X_2^2+X_3^2}\right)=0.05\)

  4. \(P\!\left(X_1^2+X_2^2>39(X_3^2+X_4^2)\right)=k\)

NoteProblem 1

Prove the memoryless property of the exponential distribution.

If

\[ X\sim\operatorname{Exp}(\lambda), \]

show that

\[ P(X>s+t\mid X>s)=P(X>t),\qquad s,t>0. \]

NoteProblem 2

Let \(X\) and \(Y\) be two jointly continuous random variables with joint PDF: \[f_{XY}(x,y)=\begin{cases} x+cy^2, \quad 0\leq x\leq 1, 0\leq y \leq 1 \\ 0, \quad \quad \quad \text{otherwise} \end{cases}\]

  1. Find the constant \(c\).

  2. Find P(\(0\leq X\leq 0.5, 0\leq Y\leq 0.5\)).

3 Category 3

CautionProblem 1

Let \(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n\) be independent random variables with standard normal distribution. Prove that the random variable \(\eta=\xi_1^2+\ldots+\xi_n^2\) has distribution \(\Gamma(1/2,n/2)\).

CautionProblem 2

The CDF of a random vector \((\xi, \eta)\) is given by

\[\begin{equation*} F_{\xi,\eta}(x,y) = \left\{ \begin{array}{lr} 0 & , x \leq 0 \ \text{or} \ y\leq 0,;\\ 1-e^{-x^2}-e^{-2y}+e^{-x^2-2y} & , x\geq 0, y\geq 0. \end{array} \right. \end{equation*}\]

Find

  1. The joint PDF \(p_{\xi, \eta}(x,y)\).

  2. The probabilities \({\sf P}(-2\leq \xi <2, 1\leq \eta <3)\), \({\sf P}(\xi \geq 0, \eta \geq 1)\), \({\sf P}(-2\leq \xi <2, 1\leq \eta <3)\), \({\sf P}( \xi <1, \eta \geq 2)\)

  3. CDFs \(F_{\xi}\) and \(F_{\eta}\).

CautionProblem 3

Let \(\xi_1, \xi_2\) be independent random variables, uniformly distributed on \([0,2]\). Find \({\sf P}(1\leq \xi_2 \xi_2\leq 2)\).
